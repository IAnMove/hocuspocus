Use a template

Install it from My templates → Community in HocusPocus, or download the .hptemplate and use Import. Fill its slots with your own characters and backgrounds.

Share yours

Save your shot as a template in HocusPocus, download it and open a pull request adding it as templates/<your-handle>/<slug>.hptemplate in the community repo.

Safe by design

Templates are data only. Every package is validated before publishing and HocusPocus checks its SHA-256 before importing it.
