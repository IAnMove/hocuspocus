[Verse]
Beneath an ashen sun I crossed dead kingdoms.
Their hollow bells were whispering your name.
My princess slept behind a gate of bone.
I swore the dark would give you back again.

[Chorus]
Open, night, your hungry jaws.
Let my love draw breath once more.
If one heart can buy the morning,
Take the beating heart I bore.

[Verse]
The eyeless king demanded one last bargain.
Your soul for hers. No road will lead you home.
I signed in blood and broke her silver fetters.
She reached the sea. I faced the dark alone.

[Bridge]
Do not turn when silence takes my heartbeat.
Do not make your grief another cage.
I smiled to see you walking through the sunrise.
Death took my hand. You lived. The debt was paid.

[Outro]
Beneath an ashen sun the sea lay waking.
My name upon your lips. My sword beneath the waves.
