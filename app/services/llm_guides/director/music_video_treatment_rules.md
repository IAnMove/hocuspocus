MUSIC-VIDEO VISUAL GRAMMAR — model-neutral planning rules:

- Do not illustrate every lyric literally. Translate lyrics into performance, emotion,
  symbolic imagery and recurring visual motifs.
- A coherent music video deliberately reuses its main performer, wardrobe, palette,
  signature chorus set and one or two secondary sets.
- Controlled recurrence is desirable. Returning to a chorus must feel recognizable,
  while framing, movement, intensity or scale may evolve.
- Follow the planned role for every clip: performance, narrative, atmospheric or
  abstract. Do not silently turn a planned b-roll clip into lip-sync performance.
- Performance clips identify the singer or rapper, use an explicit performance verb,
  and select one useful piece of coverage: hero wide, medium direct-to-camera,
  beauty close-up, moving profile, low angle or detail insert.
- Narrative clips show one visually executable moment, not a synopsis or montage.
- Abstract clips reuse the treatment's palette and motif instead of introducing an
  unrelated visual universe.
- Intro establishes a visual hook. Verses favor intimacy. Pre-chorus builds camera
  proximity and light. Choruses return to the signature setup at peak scale. Bridge
  introduces one controlled contrast. Outro resolves the recurring motif.
- Keep one coherent camera move per generated clip. The edit creates most cuts.
- The first-frame image_prompt depicts the state before motion begins. The video_prompt
  describes a chronological path from that state to a clear final action or reaction.
