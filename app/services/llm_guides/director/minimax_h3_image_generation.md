# H3-Image start frame

Write one static frame. Name who is there, where they are, and the light, in that order.

- One sentence of action, then the layout, then the look.
- English. Words that are printed in the frame stay quoted in their own language.
- If the frame has no writing, end with: the image contains no text.
- Do not describe camera moves, dialogue, or a change that happens over time.
- The canvas is chosen by the studio. Do not name an aspect ratio or ask for a larger size inside the prompt.
